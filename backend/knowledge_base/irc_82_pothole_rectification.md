# IRC:82-2015 Code of Practice for Maintenance of Bituminous Surfaces

## Section 1: Overview and Classification of Potholes
According to municipal highway standards (IRC:82-2015 Section 4.1), surface depressions and potholes are classified into three severity tiers based on depth and surface area:
- **Shallow Defects (Low Priority)**: Depth under 25 mm, surface area under 0.1 m². Typical of early asphalt raveling or surface loss.
- **Moderate Defects (Medium Priority)**: Depth between 25 mm and 50 mm, surface area between 0.1 m² and 0.5 m². Surface layer compromised, base layer intact.
- **Severe / Deep Craters (High / Critical Priority)**: Depth exceeding 50 mm, surface area exceeding 0.5 m², or any defect exposing the granular sub-base or with pooled standing water.

## Section 2: Rectangular Edge Cutting and Excavation (Clause 4.2)
1. **Delineation**: The repair boundary must be delineated as a clean rectangle or square extending at least 50 mm (2 inches) into sound, uncracked surrounding pavement.
2. **Vertical Cuts**: Edges must be saw-cut vertically using a diamond-blade asphalt saw or sharp jackhammer chisel. Sloped or feathered edges are strictly prohibited as they lead to premature edge fraying.
3. **Debris Removal**: The excavated cavity must be thoroughly cleaned of loose asphalt chunks, dust, dirt, and mud using compressed air or stiff wire brooms.
4. **Base Compaction**: The exposed base course must be inspected. If moisture is present, base must be dried, and granular material re-compacted to at least 98% modified Proctor density before applying bituminous mixes.

## Section 3: Tack Coat Application (Clause 4.3)
1. **Emulsion Grade**: Apply rapid-setting cationic bitumen emulsion complying with IS:8887 (Grade RS-1) or rapid-curing cutback bitumen.
2. **Application Rate**: Uniformly spray tack coat over the bottom and all vertical side walls of the excavated rectangle at a rate of 0.25 to 0.30 kg/m².
3. **Break Time**: Allow the emulsion to 'break' (color change from brown to black) before placing any bituminous patch material. Do not pool excess emulsion in corners.

## Section 4: Hot Mix Bituminous Patching Protocol (Clause 4.4)
- **Applicability**: Standard dry weather conditions when ambient temperature is above 10°C (50°F).
- **Material Specification**: Bituminous Concrete (BC Grade 2) or Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM) prepared with Bitumen Viscosity Grade VG-30 (or VG-40 for heavy commercial corridors).
- **Placement Temperature**: The patch mixture must arrive and be laid at a minimum temperature of 130°C to 150°C.
- **Lifting Rule**: If cavity depth exceeds 75 mm, material must be laid and compacted in two distinct lifts (layers), allowing primary layer compaction before the surface wear course.
- **Compaction**: Compact immediately using an 8-to-10 ton vibratory steel roller or heavy vibratory plate compactor (minimum 20 kN centrifugal force). Compact from outer perimeter inward until no roller indentations are visible.
- **Curing & Traffic Release**: Maintain cordoned cooling for 3 to 4 hours, or until surface temperature drops below 50°C before opening to traffic.

## Section 5: Cold Mix Emergency Patching for Rainy / Wet Weather (Clause 4.5)
- **Applicability**: Monsoon seasons, damp pavement, standing puddles, or emergency repairs where hot mix plants are non-operational.
- **Material Specification**: Proprietary Cold Mix Asphalt Emulsion (CMAE) or Tailored Pre-mix Cold Patch complying with IRC:116 specifications.
- **Procedure**:
  1. Pump or sweep out free water from the pothole.
  2. While dry substrate is preferred, cold patch polymers adhere to moist aggregate.
  3. Place cold mix with a 15% surcharge (crown) to account for post-compaction settling under wheel loads.
  4. Hand-tamp or drive a vehicle wheel over the patch 3 to 4 times for initial consolidation.
- **Traffic Release**: Immediate low-speed traffic release (within 15 minutes of compaction). Recommended for temporary stabilization until dry season hot mix restoration.
