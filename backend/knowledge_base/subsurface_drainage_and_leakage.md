# Municipal Drainage, Subsurface Water Leakage & Trench Reinstatement SOP

## Section 1: Water Intrusion and Pavement Integrity
Water is the primary catalyst for rapid pavement degradation. When surface or underground water infiltrates asphalt pavement layers:
- Pore pressure from heavy vehicle wheel passages strips bitumen binder from aggregate (stripping phenomenon).
- Subgrade soil becomes liquid-plastic, losing load-bearing capacity and causing immediate subsidence craters.

## Section 2: Municipal Utility Leakage & Pipe Burst Protocol (Clause 6.1)
1. **Utility Coordination**: Contact the municipal water utility / pipeline authority immediately to isolate and depressurize leaking potable or sewage pipelines before beginning road restoration.
2. **Cavity Inspection**: Water leaks frequently wash away sub-base aggregate, forming subsurface sinkholes under seemingly intact asphalt bridges. Inspect with ground-penetrating radar or test-pit probing.
3. **Excavation & Shoring**: Cut asphalt square 300 mm beyond the perimeter of underlying cavity. If depth exceeds 1.2 m, deploy trench shoring boxes for technician safety.
4. **Bedding and Backfill**:
   - Pipe bedding: Coarse sand or 10 mm pea gravel 150 mm below and above pipeline.
   - Sub-base backfill: Granular sub-base (GSB Grade I) or crushed stone aggregate (40 mm down) laid in compacted lifts not exceeding 150 mm thickness.
   - Compaction requirement: Each lift must achieve minimum 98% maximum dry density (MDD) verified with dynamic cone penetrometer (DCP) or nuclear gauge.

## Section 3: Subsurface Drainage Remediation (Clause 6.2)
- Where chronic water accumulation occurs due to high water table:
  1. Construct edge french drains along the shoulder.
  2. Install 100 mm diameter perforated PVC or corrugated HDPE pipe wrapped in non-woven needle-punched geotextile filter fabric (class I).
  3. Backfill trench with clean 20 mm single-sized drainage gravel to prevent silt clogging.
  4. Ensure minimum 1% longitudinal gradient toward city storm drainage outfalls.
