# Standard Operating Procedure: Asphalt Crack Sealing & Structural Resurfacing

## Section 1: Crack Types and Failure Diagnostics
Road cracks indicate thermal movement, structural fatigue, or subgrade moisture intrusion:
- **Hairline & Thermal Transverse Cracks (< 3 mm width)**: Result of seasonal asphalt shrinkage and thermal cycling. Structural capacity remains intact.
- **Medium Longitudinal & Joint Cracks (3 mm to 6 mm width)**: Frequent along construction joints and wheel paths. High risk of water entry into granular sub-base.
- **Fatigue & Alligator Cracking (> 6 mm interconnected web)**: Resembles crocodile skin pattern. Indicates progressive shear failure of the underlying subgrade/base under repetitive traffic axle loads.

## Section 2: Hot-Poured Polymer Crack Sealing Protocol (Clause 5.2)
1. **Routing and Preparation**: For cracks between 3 mm and 15 mm, route a rectangular reservoir (typically 12 mm wide by 12 mm deep) to give the sealant an optimal depth-to-width ratio (1:1).
2. **Thermal Cleaning**: Clean and dry the reservoir using a high-pressure hot-compressed-air lance (minimum 6 bar pressure, 1500°C flame velocity without burning asphalt binder). Moisture in the crack prevents sealant adhesion.
3. **Sealant Material**: Use Hot-Poured Elastomeric Polymer-Modified Bitumen sealant conforming to ASTM D6690 Type II.
4. **Application**: Inject sealant from bottom up to avoid air trapping. Finish with a flush strike-off or slight overband (under 25 mm width and 2 mm thickness).
5. **Anti-Tracking Agent**: Dust freshly placed sealant with fine limestone dust or volcanic sand to prevent tire pick-up by passing vehicles.

## Section 3: Slurry Surfacing and Micro-Surfacing (Clause 5.3)
- **Applicability**: Widespread non-structural surface cracking, oxidation, or skid resistance loss.
- **Specification**: Quick-setting cationic polymer-modified bitumen emulsion mixed with graded aggregate (0-4 mm or 0-6 mm) and mineral filler (ordinary portland cement 1-2%).
- **Curing Time**: Fast-traffic return within 45 to 60 minutes under ambient temperatures above 20°C.

## Section 4: Alligator Cracking Structural Remediation (Clause 5.4)
Crack sealants cannot bridge alligator fatigue cracking.
- **Mandatory Procedure**: Full-depth mill-and-fill operation.
  1. Mill out the entire distressed surface down to sound uncompromised layer (minimum 50 mm - 100 mm depth).
  2. If sub-base exhibits pumping or clay saturation, excavate saturated base and replace with Wet Mix Macadam (WMM) or Crushed Stone Aggregate.
  3. Lay a biaxial polypropylene geogrid or paving fabric over the interface to prevent reflective cracking.
  4. Lay dense bituminous macadam base course followed by bituminous concrete wear layer.
