# Municipal Work Order SLA, Crew Safety & Traffic Cordon Bylaws

## Section 1: Severity-Based SLA Triage Deadlines (Clause 7.1)
Municipal work order dispatchers must prioritize hazard rectification according to strict service level agreements:
- **Priority Tier 1: CRITICAL HAZARD**
  - Criteria: Pothole depth > 75 mm on arterial roads, open sinkhole, uncovered utility pit, or hazard causing vehicular swerving.
  - Cordon & Taper Setup: Within **2 hours** of dispatch.
  - Temporary or Permanent Repair Completion: Within **4 hours**.
- **Priority Tier 2: HIGH HAZARD**
  - Criteria: Pothole depth 50 mm - 75 mm, active water ponding on primary bus routes, alligator cracking web > 2 m².
  - Repair Completion SLA: Within **24 hours**.
- **Priority Tier 3: MEDIUM HAZARD**
  - Criteria: Pothole depth 25 mm - 50 mm, longitudinal cracks 3 mm - 6 mm on secondary feeder roads.
  - Repair Completion SLA: Within **72 hours** (3 business days).
- **Priority Tier 4: LOW HAZARD**
  - Criteria: Surface raveling, shallow potholes < 25 mm, hairline cracks.
  - Repair Completion SLA: Within **7 calendar days** or scheduled maintenance cycle.

## Section 2: Work Zone Traffic Management & Safety Barricading (Clause 7.2)
Worker safety within live traffic corridors requires strict compliance with MUTCD / IRC:SP:55 guidelines:
1. **Advance Warning Zone**: Position "Road Work Ahead" and "Men at Work" diamond warning signs at least 150 meters upstream on expressways and 50 meters on urban streets.
2. **Transition Taper**: Place fluorescent retro-reflective traffic cones (minimum 750 mm height with two white reflective bands) along a 1:10 taper ratio.
3. **Buffer Space**: Maintain a 10-meter empty longitudinal safety buffer between the end of the taper and the first road crew vehicle.
4. **PPE Compliance**: All personnel within 15 meters of live traffic must wear EN ISO 20471 Class 3 high-visibility fluorescent vests and steel-toed puncture-resistant boots.
5. **Night Works**: When working between 20:00 and 06:00, illuminate work zone with glare-free balloon light towers delivering minimum 200 Lux at pavement surface.

## Section 3: Contractor Quality Assurance & Core Extraction (Clause 7.3)
- All asphalt patches exceeding 2.0 m² require a core extraction density test (minimum 3 cores per 100 m²).
- In-place density must be minimum 97% of theoretical maximum specific gravity (Gmm).
- Contractor warranty: 24 months for hot-mix bituminous concrete; contractor is liable for free replacement if rutting or edge raveling exceeds 5 mm during warranty period.
